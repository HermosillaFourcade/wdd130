Rafting Río Bueno

If you love adventure and enjoy whitewater rafting, this experience is for you! Come and participate in this unforgettable adventure with a great team that will accompany you on this journey down the Bueno River, from its source at Lake Ranco to its main swimming area. We'll be waiting for you!.

History

Determined to showcase the natural beauty, the rapids, and the flora and fauna of this corner of the world, so you can enjoy an unforgettable experience, this company was formed to allow everyone to enjoy and appreciate this journey.

We are an adventure company with over 20 years of experience in these types of journeys. We have the best guides in the area who know the route very well, so you can have confidence and security on this adventure.
