San Pablo, Chile

San Pablo is a Chilean commune and town located in the Los Lagos Region, within the Osorno Province. Rural and agricultural in character, it is known for its dairy and livestock production, its history linked to German and Chilote colonization, and its role as a connection point between Osorno and the Los Ríos Region
